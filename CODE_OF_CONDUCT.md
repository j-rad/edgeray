# Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of the **EdgeRay** project, we are committed to fostering an inclusive, professional, and welcoming community. This Code of Conduct applies to both online and offline participation.

## 🤝 Our Standards

* **Be Welcoming**: Use inclusive language and be kind to others, regardless of their experience level.
* **Be Respectful**: Disagreement is part of development, but personal attacks, harassment, or exclusionary behavior will not be tolerated.
* **Focus on the Technical**: Critiques should be directed at code and architecture, not at individuals.
* **Communicate Clearly**: Be professional in issues, pull requests, and any community forums.

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, including offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion.
* The use of sexualized language or imagery.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission (doxing).

## 🛡️ Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Individuals who do not follow the Code of Conduct may be temporarily or permanently barred from the project.

## 📧 Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers at **contact@edgeray.io**. All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.
